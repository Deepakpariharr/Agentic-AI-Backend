Lumen Notes - Frequently Asked Questions

Billing and Plans
Lumen Notes offers three plans: Free, Pro at 9 dollars per month, and Team at 15 dollars per user per month. The Pro plan includes unlimited notes and 10 gigabytes of file storage, while the Free plan is limited to 200 notes and 200 megabytes of storage. All paid plans are billed monthly by default, but switching to annual billing gives a 20 percent discount. Refunds are available within 14 days of a charge if requested through the billing settings page.

Account Management
You can change the email address on your account from the Account Settings page by clicking Change Email and verifying the new address through a confirmation link. If you lose access to your account and cannot receive the verification email, support can manually verify your identity using a government-issued ID, which typically takes 2 to 3 business days. Deleting your account permanently removes all notes after a 30-day grace period, during which the account can still be recovered.

Data Export
Notes can be exported at any time from Settings > Export Data, either as individual Markdown files or as a single ZIP archive containing all notes and attachments. Exports larger than 5,000 notes are processed asynchronously, and you will receive an email with a download link within 24 hours. Exported data does not include version history, which is only available while the note is stored in Lumen Notes.

Security and Privacy
All notes are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. Two-factor authentication can be enabled from Settings > Security using either an authenticator app or SMS, though SMS-based 2FA is being phased out by the end of 2025 in favor of authenticator apps only. Lumen Notes does not sell user data to third parties and only shares data with subprocessors listed on the company's public subprocessor page.

Integrations
Lumen Notes integrates natively with Google Calendar, Slack, and Zapier. The Slack integration allows notes to be created directly from any Slack channel using the /lumen command, and requires a Team plan or higher. The public API used for custom integrations is rate-limited to 60 requests per minute per account on the Pro plan and 300 requests per minute on the Team plan.

Support Channels
Free plan users can get help through the community forum and the help center articles. Pro plan users receive email support with a target first response time of 24 hours. Team plan customers additionally get access to live chat support during business hours, Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time.
