Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Section 1: Time Off Policy
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly starting from the first day of employment. Unused vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Vacation requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 10 business days in advance for approval by a direct manager. In addition to vacation, employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, which do not carry over and do not require advance notice.

Section 2: Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with prior written approval from their manager. Fully remote arrangements require sign-off from both the manager and the VP of the employee's department. Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours of 10 AM to 4 PM in their local time zone. The company provides a one-time home office stipend of 500 dollars for remote equipment such as monitors and chairs.

Section 3: Health and Retirement Benefits
Acme Corp covers 90 percent of the premium for the base medical, dental, and vision plans for employees, and 60 percent of the premium for dependents. The company offers a 401(k) retirement plan with a 4 percent matching contribution, fully vested after two years of continuous employment. Open enrollment for benefits occurs every year in November.

Section 4: Expense Reimbursement Policy
Business-related expenses must be submitted through the expense system within 30 days of the purchase date, accompanied by an itemized receipt. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 75 dollars per day. Any single expense over 200 dollars requires pre-approval from a department director before it is incurred. Reimbursements are processed within two pay cycles of approval.

Section 5: Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism at all times. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. All employees must complete annual compliance training on workplace conduct, data privacy, and anti-bribery laws within 60 days of their start date and every year thereafter.

Section 6: Termination and Notice Period
Employees who wish to resign are asked to provide at least 2 weeks of written notice to their manager. In cases of involuntary termination not related to misconduct, Acme Corp provides severance pay equal to one week of base salary for every year of service, with a minimum of 4 weeks. Final paychecks, including any accrued and unused vacation, are issued within 7 business days of the employee's last day.
